Fill the Word

Interactive prototype

Tap a word, then tap a blank — dragging still works but is optional. One difficulty scale runs through both modes: Easy, Medium, Hard, By Heart, blanking a quarter, half, three quarters and then every word of the verse. Switch difficulty or change the verse right on the game screen. Casual is a single round; Memorize takes a passage up the ladder, and after each level you choose to go deeper, carry the same level to the next verse, or run it again.
